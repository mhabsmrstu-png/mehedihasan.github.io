About Me

I am a researcher in Systems and Information Engineering at the University of Tsukuba, Japan. My research develops representations of time-series data from nonlinear dynamical systems, in particular recurrence triangles, which capture fine-scale structure in recurrence plots.

I apply these methods to supervised and unsupervised time-series analysis, and to real-world gait data, including the classification of Parkinson's disease patients from their walking behavior and the clustering of gait by age group.

Research Interests

Methods for understanding complex temporal behavior.

Recurrence Triangles

Triangular motifs in recurrence plots as interpretable features of local recurrence geometry.

Nonlinear Dynamics

Analyzing temporal behavior in chaotic and nonlinear systems such as the Rössler and Lorenz models.

Time-Series Classification & Clustering

Small-sample and unsupervised methods for distinguishing temporal patterns.

Human Gait Analysis

Gait dynamics for detecting Parkinson's disease and characterizing age-related differences.
